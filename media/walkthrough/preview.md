# Preview and SyncTeX

The PDF opens beside the source after the first successful build and reloads on every build without losing its place.

- **Source → PDF:** **⌘⌥J** (Ctrl+Alt+J) jumps to the PDF location of the cursor.
- **PDF → source:** **⌘-click** (Ctrl+click) on the PDF jumps to that line in the source.

Open the PDF any time with **⌘⌥V** (Ctrl+Alt+V).
